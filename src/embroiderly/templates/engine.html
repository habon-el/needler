Stitch generation

Generate stitches from prepared artwork. To recolour or remove threads from an existing design without losing its border, open its saved stitch preview.

Generation uses the current prepared artwork and its supported settings. Existing custom demonstration borders are not recreated by this form. Results need review and a sew-out.
