Choose your threads

Uncheck a colour to remove its stitches, or choose a replacement colour. This applies to every occurrence of that colour, including borders sharing it. Removed stitches leave fabric exposed; other colours are not expanded to fill the gaps.

Preview changes do not overwrite this design. Removing underlay or supporting colours may affect the sew-out.
