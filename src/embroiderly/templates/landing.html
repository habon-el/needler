BEFORE YOUR FIRST STITCH

A few good
things to know.

What can I upload?+

PNG, JPG, WebP or BMP images up to 12 MB. Clear logos and simple artwork are a good starting point. Detailed photographs need more preparation and careful review.

Does the preview guarantee the sewn result?+

No. Fabric, stabiliser, thread, tension and your machine affect the result. The stitch preview helps you review the plan; a physical sew-out is still needed.

Can I make raised or 3D embroidery?+

Experimental layered satin is available for supported narrow shapes. Complex lettering and irregular areas are limited. It is not foam embroidery, and the preview does not predict physical height.

Are the cap and machine photos actual results?+

They are AI-generated concept images for inspiration, not customer work or products sewn by Embroiderly. The interactive “hello” sample uses actual generated stitch-file paths.
